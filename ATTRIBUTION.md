# Sample catalog and image attribution

This portfolio snapshot contains an original, synthetic 120-product demonstration catalog and 18 code-authored geometric SVG illustrations in `public/sample-images/`. The shapes and generic names are placeholders for UI exploration, not depictions or inventory claims for branded goods.

The source storefront used externally sourced product photography and generated recolor previews. Those photographs, generated variants, upstream catalog entries, public external image URLs, image-account file IDs and operational provenance records are excluded from this snapshot. The user's original source and live deployment were preserved.

Illustrated colors are marked with `generatedPreview` in the seed and labeled in the product interface. All seeded products are marked `sample: true`. No sample image is a product photograph. Real merchant images should be independently owned or authorized.

Replace sample imagery and catalog entries with material you own or are authorized to use before running a commercial store. No project-wide open-source license has been added.
